# Arcarum and Evoker research

This note contains reusable Arcarum/Evoker material guidance. Element-specific
priorities remain authoritative in `notes/elements/`.

## Female-only account sequence

**Haaselia → Fraux → Nier → Maria Theresa**

Recruitment may happen before the previous Evoker reaches 5★ when Evolite and
recruitment materials are spare, but the active 5★ project should not be
delayed.

Current account targets are tracked interactively on `/guides/arcarum`:

- Haaselia: weapon 4★, Domain 1/4, eventual Lv110 reserve.
- Fraux: weapon 0★, Domain 0/4, announced October Lv110 reserve.
- Nier and Maria Theresa: unrecruited, stop at 5★ for now.
- Caim: weapon 1★; finish only the weapon to 5★ for the Diaspora entry route.
  Fourth skill and Transcendence remain Earth-main/manual investments.

At those defaults the plan has **435 confirmed NWQ remaining**, plus **40 projected
NWQ** for the two selected Lv110 reserves, or **475 NWQ total**. The projected
portion is deliberately separated because Fraux is not released yet and
Haaselia's Transcendence batch is not announced.

Alanaan, Geisenborger, Lobelia, Estarriola and Katzelia live in a separate
inactive reserve. They enter the total only when an element-specific endgame
plan justifies enabling them.

## Typical Domain plus Foundation weapon 5★ materials

Approximate total for one Evoker:

- Corresponding Veritas: **720**
- Corresponding Astra: **550**
- Corresponding Ideas: **440**
- Corresponding Brights: **250**
- Vellum Documents: **1,060**
- New World Quartz: **90**
- Time Sands: **3**

The usual practical gates are Veritas first, then Ideas and Astra. For Haaselia,
prioritize Moon Veritas and Moon Ideas. Farm Mundus five-gauge Water enemies and
Sephira boxes together, use monthly Arcarum exchange stock and corresponding
Arcarum Gaiden stock, and do not spend New World Quartz to shortcut Astra farming.

## Xeno Lucky Loot

While the female-only Evoker plan is active, select **Sephira Evolite** from
Xeno Lucky Loot. Xeno Cocytus Militis and Xeno Vohu Manah Militis can provide
Evolite. Use whichever boss is faster and safer; Cocytus is the natural
Water-aligned choice for this account. Gold Bricks or Lapis Merits become better
only after planned Evokers are recruited or Evolite is no longer the bottleneck.

## Transcendence status

- Maria Theresa received the first Evoker Transcendence release on **21 July
  2026**, initially allowing Lv110.
- The next announced October 2026 batch is **Nier, Estarriola and Fraux**.
- Maria's older C-tier investment priority mainly describes the pre-Transcendence
  5★ table. Her 6★ is substantially more relevant to Water high-difficulty and
  dispel-heavy teams.
- Do not assume dates for the remaining Evokers until officially announced.
