# Eternal Investment Plan

Last reviewed: 2026-09-08  
Profile: casual Full Auto, female-only Transcendence investment, with Seox excluded by preference.

## Account rule

- Recruit every male Eternal only to the base 4-star state for now.
- Do not spend a Gold Brick, Revenant Weapon fragments, blue paper or other major materials on a male Eternal's 5-star uncap or Transcendence until this policy is revisited.
- Reserve all 5-star and Transcendence investment for the five female Eternals: Fif, Threo, Tien, Tweyen and Niyon.
- Character ratings are not the same as material priority. Stop at the useful breakpoint instead of taking every project automatically to level 150.

## Female-only Transcendence sequence

1. **Fif: level 130 first; level 150 when Light high difficulty or solo play justifies it.**
   - Kamigame priority: 5/5.
   - Level 120 adds party turn-damage reduction; level 130 adds Dispel Cancel and anti-Undead/Curse support; level 150 makes her recovery/restore button reusable.
2. **Threo: level 130 offensive breakpoint; level 150 only for sustained use.**
   - Kamigame priority: 4/5.
   - Level 120 strengthens her low-HP normal damage; level 130 adds her important three-action turn; level 150 improves long-fight stability.
3. **Tien: level 120 account-utility breakpoint.**
   - Kamigame priority: 3/5.
   - Level 120 raises maximum Treasure Hunt to 10. Stop there while Grand Zeta and other modern Fire attackers cover combat; levels 130–150 are optional if Tien becomes an active Fire frontline project.
4. **Tweyen: level 110 economical Full Auto breakpoint.**
   - Kamigame priority: 2/5.
   - Continue to 130 or 150 only when her debuffs, skill damage, hit count and repeated fourth ability solve an actual Light encounter.
5. **Niyon: remain level 100 unless committing directly to level 150.**
   - Kamigame priority: 2/5.
   - Level 150 is the meaningful destination for permanent damage-cap support, normal-attack amplification and partywide Dispel Cancel. Her role overlaps modern Wind supports, so she is not an automatic early project.

## Male Eternals: recruitment only

- **Anre, Feower, Seox, Seofon and Eahta:** recruit to base 4-star only.
- This is an account resource policy, not a claim that all five are weak. Kamigame rates Seox, Seofon and Eahta highly after Transcendence, but their material cost is outside the active plan.
- Seox is specifically excluded from Dark team planning. Dark progression uses Nier/The Death plus Grand Lich, Grand Sariel, Grand Orologia, Summer Magisa, Tsukuyomi, Tyra, Summer Azusa and other non-Seox options.
