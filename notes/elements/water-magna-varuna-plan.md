# GBF Water — Magna to Varuna Plan

Last updated: 2026-09-13

Revans update: the owned Wamdus Kengo route is explicitly semi-Full Auto;
routine Mugen Full Auto waits for Defense Schrodingers and Haaselia 5-star
skill 4. See the [Revans research note](../research/revans-research.md#mugen--water).
Profile: casual, Full Auto oriented, with a single-sided Varuna entry and optional double-Varuna endpoint.

## Account core and teams

Owned core includes Payila, Grand Gabriel, Grand Octavia, Grand Yatima, Grand Sylvia, Wamdus, Grand Poseidon, Summer Lucio and Cupitan. Varuna is not yet owned; a base Gabriel summon is owned.

### Missing gacha targets

1. **Grand Europa — first Water Flash spark target.** Her 5-star kit is the broadest missing Full Auto upgrade: recurring healing and clear, Fire switch, dispel guard, strong personal normal damage, and long-fight reattack support. She can replace Octavia when survival and consistency matter more than Octavia's repeat-attack damage. Galilei's Insight also fills one of the planned Varuna transition slots.
2. **Yukata Alliah — seasonal high-difficulty comfort target.** Prioritize only when near-every-turn dispel, healing, cover utility and low-intervention mechanic handling are the actual goal.
3. **Summer Tefnut — seasonal medium/long Full Auto speed target.** Strong, but the owned roster already functions without her.
4. **Grand Pholia — weapon-conditioned Legfest target, not a parallel Europa priority.** Her current character value is niche and barrier-dependent. Spark her only when the exact Varuna grid is ready and still needs Taisai Spirit Bow; do not force the spark merely to complete the character collection.
5. **Water Zeta — normal-pool Suptix speed specialist.** Current short-fight guides use her, but she is not required for Team A and should not consume a Gala spark.

Grand Europa and Grand Pholia are not interchangeable spark targets: Europa is a direct character-and-weapon upgrade on Flash Gala, while Pholia is mainly a Legfest weapon decision. Grand Lancelot remains a later skill-archetype target and Vajra remains a later Kengo specialist.

### Awakening types

- Payila: **Multiattack**. Guaranteed TA wastes the DATA portion, but the 5% normal-attack amplification applies to every normal action and reattack. Use Attack only as a temporary under-cap option on a weak grid.
- Grand Gabriel: **Multiattack**. TA directly builds Pargos for recurring automatic damage and dispel; normal-attack amplification remains useful afterward.
- Grand Octavia: **Multiattack** in Team A. The normal-attack amplification scales her repeated attacks and post-CA assassin turn. Attack is only an under-cap fallback.
- Grand Yatima: **Defense** for medium/long and high-difficulty fights. Balance is acceptable when conserving awakening orbs; Multiattack is a fixed-route damage specialization.
- Grand Sylvia: **Defense** for the owned Kengo/high-difficulty role. Attack is limited to safe CA farming where survival is already solved.

## Practical single-sided Varuna entry

Use an owned route-defining main summon—such as Yatima, Beelzebub, Orologia or Versusia when its specific effect is useful—and borrow a level-250 Varuna. Personal Varuna is not required to activate the Primal grid.

| Slot     | Weapon                                                              |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mainhand | Staff of Repudiation, or Magna Staff of Renunciation with Falsehood |
| 2–3      | Rubea Stiria ×2                                                     |
| 4        | Taisai Spirit Bow                                                   |
| 5        | Galilei's Insight                                                   |
| 6        | Gospel of Water and Sky                                             |
| 7        | Wamdus's Cnidocyte                                                  |
| 8        | Knight of Ice or defensive flex                                     |
| 9        | Ultima Staff                                                        |
| 10       | Celestial, Destroyer or encounter flex                              |

Modern Varuna commonly uses only one Wamdus spear. Rubea Stiria boost weapons, Gabriel's passive and the remaining Varuna-boosted weapons let a single borrowed aura reach useful thresholds; one Wamdus can finish critical rate and contributes 50,000 crit supplemental damage. Verify 100% critical for the exact one-sided preset rather than copying a double-sided grid unchanged.

### Transition threshold

1. Gospel of Water and Sky ×1
2. Galilei's Insight ×1
3. Taisai Spirit Bow ×1
4. Rubea Stiria ×2
5. An owned main summon whose aura or opening call the saved route actually uses

Stay Magna until most of this package is ready. One Yatima weapon and one Gabriel weapon alone are not enough reason to spend bars and transition. Test the completed package with support Varuna 250 before investing in a personal Varuna transcendence.

### Double Varuna remains a later option

Personal Varuna 250 is valuable when a repeated encounter needs boosted HP, Garrison, healing, TA or easier critical thresholds, and it removes dependence on finding the correct support. At level 250 it can also provide 20% Water elemental ATK and HP from a sub slot when another summon is main; this sub aura does not boost Primal weapon skills. These are flexibility benefits, not prerequisites for the first Primal transition.

## Extreme-investment alternative

The specialized ceiling is a Hraesvelgr normal-attack grid:

- Hraesvelgr mainhand
- Rubea Stiria ×2
- Bridekeeper ×2
- Clockwork Blade: Kumogakure ×2
- Taisai Spirit Bow or Galilei's Insight
- Transcended Staff of Repudiation
- Destroyer weapon
- Ultima/Celestial pieces in extra slots

It requires Soldier, bullets, premium characters, summon support and enough TA to overcome Hraesvelgr's penalty. It is not the recommended casual Full Auto route. Clockwork Blade's Deathstrike echo can also overlap poorly with Falsehood or Haaselia, so two Octavia weapons are not automatically optimal for Manadiver.

## Summons, backline and action plan

- Current main/support: Leviathan Omega
- Primal entry: owned Yatima, Beelzebub, Orologia, Versusia or encounter utility × support Varuna 250
- Later durable option: personal Varuna 250 × support Varuna 250
- Calls: FLB Triple Zero, FLB Beelzebub, Yatima and encounter utility
- Sub auras: The Moon and Gabriel
- Farm the female-only Evoker sequence: **Haaselia/The Moon → Fraux/The Devil → Nier/The Death → Maria Theresa/Justice**.
- Haaselia is the first Water project; recruit and complete 5★ before diverting major materials.
- Recruitment may happen before the previous Evoker reaches 5★ when Evolite and recruitment materials are spare, but never at the cost of the active 5★ project.

### Water backline priorities

- **Default:** Haaselia + Grand Gabriel; use Maria Theresa, Urki, Macula Marius or a defensive flex in the remaining reserve slot.
- **Dispel-heavy high difficulty:** Manadiver / Grand Gabriel / Grand Octavia / Grand Yatima, with Haaselia + Maria Theresa in reserve.
- Replace Yatima with Grand Sylvia for a charge-attack/dispel setup.
- Replace Octavia with Sylvia when manual dispel timing and omen control matter more than automatic Full Auto dispels.
- Use Macula Marius instead of Maria for crest-based skill teams, or Summer Kolulu for crest-based normal-attack teams.
- Maria is a specialized Water high-difficulty backliner, not the default next Evoker after Haaselia.

Progression:

1. Farm two Leviathan Gaze Mare and two Leviathan Cranium Mare.
2. Move to Dark Opus mainhand with Manadiver.
3. Keep both Wamdus spears only when the active Magna III grid reaches reliable critical.
4. Stay Magna until the premium weapon package is complete, then test it single-sided with support Varuna 250.
5. Expect one Wamdus spear in eventual Varuna.
6. Ignore Hraesvelgr unless deliberately adopting a specialized manual/short-burst plan.
7. Transcend a personal Varuna only when double-sided durability, support independence or its level-250 sub aura solves a repeated need.
