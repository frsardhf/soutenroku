## How Japanese ratings should be read

- A score of 10 is not a universal statement that the character wins every team comparison.
- Ratings combine broad usefulness, current content relevance, uniqueness and performance across multiple modes.
- The 9.9–9.5 range generally contains very strong meta or near-meta characters, sometimes more specialized than a 10.
- The 9.4–9.0 range contains useful or specialist characters that can outperform a 10 in the correct grid, boss, turn count or team archetype.
- Always compare the mode tags: short farming, Full Auto and high difficulty.
- Grid strength, Magna versus Primal, boss mechanics, CA On/Off and battle length can reverse the numerical order.
