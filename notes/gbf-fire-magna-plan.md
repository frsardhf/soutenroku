# GBF Fire Magna Plan

Last updated: 2026-08-20  
Profile: casual, Full Auto oriented, prioritizing reliable event and Guild War rewards.

> This is the authoritative Fire note. Broader background and earlier research are retained in the conversation research log.

## Team A — Grand Zeta normal attacks

- MC: Manadiver
- Grand Zeta
- Michael
- Grand Wilnas, Yukata Izmir, or Yukata Aglovale
- Charge attacks: On by default; test Off only for short farming

Grand Zeta is the main attacker. Michael supplies Fire ATK, echo, charge support, dispel and delay. Grand Wilnas is the stable self-contained attacker; Yukata Izmir is the faster offensive alternative after charging; Yukata Aglovale supplies Veil, Dispel Cancel and automatic skill damage.

After obtaining Grand Percival:

- MC / Grand Zeta / Grand Percival / Grand Wilnas or Yukata Izmir
- Move Michael to reserve when her active utility is unnecessary.

Grand Percival remains the first Fire spark target. Grand Yuel is a later improvement, not a prerequisite.

## Team B — Sandira CA/Fatal Chain

- MC: Kengo with Unsigned Kaneshige
- Sandira
- Fire Sevilbarra
- Sato
- Charge attacks: On

Michael, Yukata Izmir and Shion are substitutions. Summer Atum is the premium long-fight partner but is not required for casual rewards; Fire Sevilbarra and Sato already provide a workable engine.

## Tactical options

- Ragazzo: opening damage and a deliberate reserve swap; place Grand Wilnas or Yukata Izmir first in reserve.
- Standard Yuel: conventional teamwide DATA and Fire echo buffer.
- Athena: defensive unattended Full Auto.
- Yukata Aglovale: utility-heavy Full Auto.
- Michael: default passive reserve when not required in front.
