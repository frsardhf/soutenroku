## Two separate Fire archetypes

The account can deliberately maintain two Fire teams instead of forcing every strong character into one party.

### Team A — Grand Zeta normal-attack team

Current versions:

- MC / Grand Zeta / Michael / Grand Wilnas
- MC / Grand Zeta / Michael / Yukata Izmir
- MC / Grand Zeta / Michael / Yukata Aglovale

After Grand Percival:

- MC / Grand Zeta / Grand Percival / Grand Wilnas, Izmir or Aglovale
- Michael moves to the backline unless active dispel/delay or charge support is needed.

This is the general reward-farming and short/medium Full Auto team.

### Team B — Sandira charge-attack/FC team

Sandira's distinctive role is a CA and Fatal Chain engine, including a 200% maximum FC gauge. This team is aimed more at long Full Auto and high-difficulty/V2 content than at fast ordinary farming.

Best currently owned no-Summer-Atum version:

- MC: Kengo with Unsigned Kaneshige
- Sandira
- Fire Sevilbarra
- Sato
- Backline: Michael plus Fraux/defensive flex
- Charge attacks: On

Other owned variants:

- Kengo / Sandira / Fire Sevilbarra / Michael — more dispel, delay and charge support
- Kengo / Sandira / Sato / Yukata Izmir — more CA damage and team CA supplemental damage
- Kengo / Sandira / Shion / Sato — straightforward CA damage, but less comprehensive utility
- Kengo / Sandira / Yukata Izmir / Michael — usable general CA team when Sevilbarra or Sato does not match the encounter

Summer Atum is Sandira's premium partner because Sandira and Kengo repeatedly CA, rapidly raising Atum's unique level; Atum then adds damage, gauge support and durability. For maximum Fire CA/high-difficulty potential, the standard direction is:

- Kengo / Sandira / Fire Sevilbarra / Summer Atum

However, Summer Atum is not required for casual rewards or for operating Sandira's core FC engine. The account's Fire Sevilbarra and Sato are credible substitutes. Grand Yuel is another premium future alternative, but is not required before Grand Percival.

An independently reported no-Atum full-chain-loop example used Kengo, Summer Mirin, Sandira and Yukata Izmir. The account does not currently appear to own Summer Mirin, but the example confirms that Sandira's loop is not mechanically locked to Summer Atum.

## Ragazzo consideration

Ragazzo should be treated as a deliberate frontline sacrifice/swap piece, not as a sustained echo or CA support character.

Example:

- MC / Grand Zeta / Michael / Ragazzo
- Grand Wilnas or Yukata Izmir in the first backline position

Ragazzo contributes strong early damage and is knocked out after his timed state, bringing the first backline attacker forward. This can be useful when:

- The encounter lasts long enough for the swap to occur
- The entering character is more valuable after the opening turns
- A controlled casualty does not endanger Full Auto stability

It is less attractive for very short fights that finish before the swap and for difficult unattended battles where losing a frontline member creates unnecessary risk. Ragazzo is generally a Zeta-team tactical option rather than a Sandira CA-team member.

## Owned partywide echo and DATA options

### Michael

Correction preserved from the conversation: Michael's Skill 2 affects **all Fire allies**, granting 20% Fire echo plus CA and defensive benefits until each ally has been hit three times. Michael's Skill 1 is the single-target Instant Charge and CA Reactivation skill.

### Standard Fire Yuel

At level 90+, Yuel supplies for three turns:

- 20% teamwide Fire echo
- 30% DA
- 15% TA

She is the closest owned conventional DATA/echo buffer. A usable team is Zeta/Michael/Yuel.

Yuel does not solve Izmir's initial forced single attacks or Wilnas's permanent multiattack restriction. Wilnas can only TA through his own post-CA effect.

### Other distinctions

- Yukata Izmir's post-normal-attack damage is skill damage, not echo.
- Yukata Aglovale supplies skill-damage support and utility, not party echo.
- Grand Wilnas supplies no teamwide echo or DATA; he is personal DPS/tank utility.
- Zeta largely manages her own multiattacks.

## Spark plan

The player intends to wait for an end-of-year Premium Gala/Legfest spark.

### First target: Grand Percival

Percival remains the clear priority over Grand Yuel. His value is not simply DATA:

- Powerful teamwide attack without turn progression
- Strong personal damage
- Partywide echo enabled by Fire ATK Up
- Strong synergy with Zeta
- Strong synergy with Wilnas's Singed generation
- Excellent short- and medium-duration farming value

Michael naturally supplies Fire ATK Up, helping maintain Percival's passive party echo.

### Grand Yuel later

Grand Yuel is a strong eventual improvement, but the roster already has several competent third slots: Wilnas, Yukata Izmir, Yukata Aglovale, Michael, Athena and standard Yuel. Therefore she is less urgent than Percival.

### Team progression

1. Now, damage: Zeta / Michael / Wilnas or Izmir
2. Now, utility: Zeta / Michael / Yukata Aglovale
3. After one spark: Zeta / Grand Percival / Wilnas, Izmir or Aglovale; Michael in the backline when active utility is unnecessary
4. Later balanced endpoint: Zeta / Grand Percival / Grand Yuel; Michael backline

For mechanic-heavy bosses, Michael can return to the frontline.

## Fire backline and Arcarum roadmap

### Current practical backline

- **Michael** is the default passive backline choice whenever her active frontline utility is unnecessary. Her Primarch passive boosts Fire weapon skills from either the frontline or backline.
- The other slot is encounter-dependent: Athena or another defensive replacement for Full Auto, or Grand Wilnas/Yukata Izmir behind Ragazzo so the intended attacker enters when Ragazzo leaves.

### Future Evokers

1. **Alanaan / The Sun route:** first Fire Arcarum priority for overall account value. The Sun is an exceptionally important offensive summon, while developed Alanaan is a premier burst enabler. His reverse-position passive improves healing and clears one debuff when a Fire ally takes turn-based damage; after 5-star development it gains cumulative offensive and defensive value. He may deliberately enter from reserve for burst instead of remaining there permanently.
2. **Fraux / The Devil route:** the more natural casual Full Auto and debuff-oriented project. Her reverse-position passive extends local turn-based debuffs by one turn, and developed Fraux can enter as a strong healing, dispel and control character.

Pursue **Alanaan first** because The Sun and Fire burst have broader value, then Fraux for difficult unattended Full Auto. Neither is required before using the current Fire teams.

## Character direction

### Team A — normal-attack Full Auto

- MC: Lancer Origin for optimized short/medium content, or Manadiver for a more general setup
- Payila
- Grand Gabriel
- Grand Octavia
- Backline: Haaselia plus Maria Theresa once developed; otherwise Yatima/Sylvia and a defensive flex
- Charge attacks: Off for short normal-attack farming; On when Octavia's CA/buff extension or boss survival makes it worthwhile

This trio is validated by 2026 Japanese Guild War use rather than being merely theoretical. Payila is the normal-attack/re-attack DPS and enabler; Gabriel supplies echo, TA/defense, automatic damage and dispel; Octavia adds very high normal and post-attack damage plus buff extension.

Octavia wants sufficient HP and TA support. A documented NM200 setup used Gabriel Skill 2 plus Octavia Skill 1 to reach 100% TA. If the eventual grid cannot meet Octavia's HP/TA conditions, Yatima is the easiest owned replacement until the grid improves.

Practical variants:

- Payila / Gabriel / Octavia — primary general damage team
- Payila / Gabriel / Yatima — less dependent on Octavia's grid thresholds and excellent for broad content
- Payila / Gabriel / Grand Poseidon — older but stable multi-hit/utility option
- Payila / Gabriel / Summer Lucio — burst-support alternative

### Team B — CA/high-difficulty Full Auto

The account already owns the modern foundation:

- MC: Kengo with Unsigned Kaneshige
- Grand Sylvia
- Grand Yatima
- Wamdus
- Backline: Gabriel plus Haaselia/defensive flex
- Charge attacks: On

Sylvia continuously supplies charge bar, healing, debuff-duration reduction, permanent CA-oriented team buffs and automatic effects when Water allies perform multiple CAs. Yatima supplies permanent team effects, guaranteed TA, strong damage and broad high-difficulty utility. Wamdus is the owned 200%-bar/CA-oriented durable slot.

Owned substitutions:

- Macula Marius instead of Wamdus for delay/control and safer long FA
- Payila instead of Wamdus when more raw damage is needed, though this overlaps with Team A
- Grand Poseidon for multi-hit utility and stability
- Lily or Joel when the encounter demands much more healing/cleansing

This second team should be leveled and tested before spending a spark. It is genuinely different from Team A: Team A is CA-Off normal/re-attack damage, while Team B is CA-On gauge/CA/high-difficulty play.

### Optional Team C — skill-damage Full Auto

The roster can also form a skill-focused team without new pulls:

- MC: Manadiver
- Grand Gabriel
- Cupitan 5★
- Grand Yatima or Grand Sylvia

Cupitan's 5★ kit supplies repeated seven-hit skills, skill supplemental damage and increasing dispel/heal/clear utility over time. This is attractive for long fights with hit-count and skill-damage requirements.

Grand Lancelot would be the clearest future Grand acquisition if the player specifically wants to maximize this third archetype, but he is not more urgent than obtaining and completing Varuna itself.

1. Grand Lancelot — only if deliberately building the skill-damage archetype.

### Future Evokers

The usual long-term arrangement is **Gabriel plus Haaselia** when Haaselia is intended to remain in reserve, or **Haaselia plus Maria Theresa** when Gabriel is required in front. Their correct-position entrance effects are part of their value, so they are not always permanent slot-six characters.
