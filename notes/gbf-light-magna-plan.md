# GBF Light Magna Roster Plan

Last updated: 2026-08-18

## Account direction

- Remain **Light Magna**. The character roster is already strong, while a Zeus conversion should wait for the weapon and summon inventories.
- The account owns a modern normal-attack core in **Summer Payila (Summer Haila) + Basara**. This means Nehan is an upgrade for speed, not a prerequisite for a usable Light team.
- The account also owns **Grand Cosmos**, so it can maintain a genuinely different CA/skill/utility preset without Summer Horus.
- Missing the three individually rated 10 characters does not leave Light broken. Nehan, Grand Sandalphon and Summer Horus fill different jobs; none is a universal one-for-one replacement for the others.
- Summer Payila was released only days before this update. Her placement below reflects her published kit and early Japanese discussion; exact best teams may change after more testing.

## Important owned characters

### Modern core

- **Summer Payila (Summer Haila):** normal-attack attacker/enabler. Repeated attacks and guaranteed multiattack partners accelerate her resources; she supplies offensive support, defensive utility and buff extension. She is the foundation of the fast team.
- **Basara:** primary owned normal attacker. He has guaranteed triple attacks, normal-attack amplification and echoes, and can reach flurry/double-strike turns. His guaranteed TA makes him a natural Payila partner. His CA mechanics are unusual, so he belongs primarily in the normal team rather than the CA team.
- **Grand Cosmos:** long-fight skill/CA attacker, dispeller and omen solver. Her value rises around 50% HP and in fights that reward multi-hit skills, repeated dispels and balanced offense/defense.
- **Yukata Narmaya:** CA/skill attacker and utility unit. She contributes automatic eight-hit skills, slow, reactive dispel and CA reactivation for herself and MC. Her MC-ougi follow-up also works from the backline.
- **Grand Lu Woh:** durable Full Auto support and damage option. His field and recovery-oriented utility make him preferable to a pure burst unit when the team must survive.

### Useful owned support and alternatives

- **Grand Jeanne d'Arc:** the closest owned substitute for the offensive side of Nehan. She can support a normal-burst turn and party damage, but she does not reproduce Nehan's entire package of double strike, echo/supplemental support and extreme short-fight compression.
- **Kumbhira:** strong developed Full Auto attacker/tank, especially at 5-star. She is a practical long-fight frontline choice when Cosmos does not solve the encounter by herself.
- **Grand Noa:** CA support, defensive utility and targeted CA reactivation. He is the cleanest owned support for the Cosmos CA preset.
- **Grand Cagliostro:** specialized CA/control support whose restrictions and timing make her more encounter-dependent than her rating suggests.
- **Grand Yuni:** excellent manual/high-difficulty utility, but her zero-turn buttons are a warning for strict one-button Full Auto because Full Auto does not automatically press zero-turn skills. Do not treat her as the default casual FA solution.
- **Yukata Zahlhamelina:** valuable frontline buffer when needed and an excellent reserve passive: one opening debuff immunity plus 10% Dark-damage reduction for Light allies.
- **Tikoh:** adds one All-Potion at battle start from the backline. This is valuable insurance for manual intervention, but it does not automatically heal a completely unattended Full Auto run.
- **Summer Seruel, Light Kou, Robertina, Light Ilsa, Summer Ragazzo and Utsusemi:** situational substitutes rather than immediate spark targets.

## Team 1: Summer Payila normal Full Auto without Nehan/Sandalphon

### Current default for casual unattended Full Auto

**MC Manadiver / Summer Payila / Basara / Grand Lu Woh**

- CA: **On**.
- Payila and Basara are the offensive core. Basara's guaranteed TA and stronger multiattack turns feed the normal-attack game plan that Payila rewards.
- Lu Woh is the default third member because the player's objective is reliable, unattended reward farming. His sustain and stability are more valuable here than Grand Jeanne's higher but less dependable burst contribution.
- This is the first preset to test against an unfamiliar or higher-level boss.

### Current fast/easy variation

**MC Manadiver / Summer Payila / Basara / Grand Jeanne d'Arc**

- CA: **Off** for short normal farming; turn it **On** if the encounter needs CA effects or the team stalls.
- Jeanne is the closest owned substitute for the offensive side of Nehan. Use her only when the Lu Woh version already survives comfortably and faster damage is worthwhile.
- Jeanne does not reproduce Nehan's full double-strike/echo/supplemental package and is not the universal default.

### Mechanics variation

**MC / Summer Payila / Basara / Yukata Narmaya**

Use Narmaya when reactive dispel, slow and automatic skill damage are useful. Keep CA **On** here because her kit scales with Light CAs and gives MC/self CA reactivation. This is a hybrid, not the fastest pure normal preset.

### What Nehan changes

After acquiring Nehan, the speed preset becomes:

**MC / Summer Payila / Basara / Nehan**

- Nehan replaces Jeanne/Lu Woh/Narmaya, not Payila or Basara.
- He greatly compresses damage through teamwide offensive buffs, echoes/supplemental support and additional attack actions, but his defensive drawback makes him less attractive for long unattended fights.
- Therefore Nehan is a **speed luxury with real value**, not the first repair the account needs.

## Team 2: Cosmos CA/skill Full Auto without Summer Horus

**MC Kengo / Grand Cosmos / Grand Noa / Grand Lu Woh**

- CA: **On**.
- Cosmos supplies multi-hit skill damage, dispels and encounter-mechanic coverage.
- Noa supports the CA cycle and can give targeted CA reactivation while adding defensive control.
- Lu Woh supplies the sustain that a Horus-less Cosmos composition otherwise lacks.
- This team is slower than a developed Cosmos/Summer Horus engine, but it is internally coherent and better suited to casual long Full Auto than trying to use the normal team everywhere.

Damage-oriented substitutions:

- Replace Lu Woh with **Yukata Narmaya** when the boss rewards more CAs, reactive dispel, slow and automatic skill hits.
- Replace Noa or Lu Woh with **Kumbhira 5-star** when a durable attacker/tank is more valuable than CA acceleration.
- Use **Grand Cagliostro** only when her particular CA/control mechanics solve the target; she is not the default universal third slot.
- Do not default to Grand Yuni for one-button FA. She becomes much better when manual opening inputs are allowed.

### What Summer Horus changes

Summer Horus is the premium partner for Cosmos in CA/skill-heavy and high-difficulty content. She improves charge flow, skill frequency and multi-hit/omen coverage. She would normally replace Noa or Lu Woh according to whether the fight still needs Lu Woh's sustain. Cosmos remains useful without her; the owned version is simply slower and less mechanically dense.

## The role of Grand Sandalphon

Grand Sandalphon is not merely another damage dealer. His broad frontline utility and Light Primarch weapon-skill passive give him value even when a particular fight uses a different carry. He is the best broad account upgrade among the three missing 10-rated characters.

He would usually replace the least necessary flex member:

- normal/general FA: replace Jeanne or Lu Woh depending required safety;
- Cosmos team: replace Noa or Lu Woh if his mechanics cover the fight;
- backline: retain his Primarch passive when he is not required in front.

### Planned teams after acquiring Grand Sandalphon

Primary normal/general Full Auto:

**MC Manadiver / Summer Payila / Basara / Grand Sandalphon**

- Use CA **Off** for genuinely short normal farming and **On** for medium/long general Full Auto.
- Sandalphon's partywide 20% Light echo directly strengthens Payila and Basara's repeated normal attacks. His own guaranteed-TA/flurry window, turn-without-passing attack, dispels, cumulative debuffs and defensive support make this much broader than the Jeanne version.
- This becomes the default offensive Light team; Nehan would still replace Sandalphon or Basara only when maximum short-fight compression matters more than safety and mechanics.

Long/difficult Full Auto:

**MC Manadiver / Grand Sandalphon / Grand Cosmos / Grand Lu Woh**

- Keep CA **On**. Sandalphon's ougis extend his party buff, while healing accelerates access to his strongest state; Lu Woh supplies sustain and Cosmos supplies dispels/multi-hit mechanics.
- Replace Cosmos with Summer Payila when the fight favors normal attacks, or replace Lu Woh with Grand Noa/Yukata Narmaya when more CA or skill output is required and survival is already sufficient.

If Sandalphon is not required in front, place him in reserve. His 20% boost to Light/Thunder/Divinity/Cavalier Omega weapon skills remains active from the backline and even while knocked out, so acquiring him improves both principal presets.

## Grand Lucio's current position (not owned)

Grand Lucio at **5-star, level 100** is not bad or obsolete. His 2026 rebalance/uncap package makes him a strong 9.8-class selfish/hybrid attacker:

- two turns of guaranteed TA plus 90% unique echo;
- a high-damage skill refreshed and doubled after ougi;
- after Wings level 6, permanent attack/cap/regen improvements and access to an unrecastable upgrade that adds post-normal skill damage plus five-hit end-of-turn damage;
- party barrier, strength, uplift, Light attack, Dark-damage reduction and a one-time emergency full heal/clear.

His limitation is roster competition, not weak numbers. He needs time or extra attack actions to reach Wings 6, has no regular dispel or teamwide debuff package, and most of his exceptional damage is personal. Summer Payila, Grand Sandalphon and Grand Cosmos contribute more team-level mechanics.

If acquired incidentally, practical Lucio teams would be:

- Current roster: **Manadiver / Summer Payila / Basara / Grand Lucio** for an aggressive normal/hybrid team.
- After Sandalphon: **Manadiver / Summer Payila / Grand Sandalphon / Grand Lucio** when personal damage and sustain matter more than Basara's faster opening.
- Keep Basara over Lucio for the shortest normal farm; use Lucio over Basara as the fight becomes long enough for Wings 6 and his level-100 permanent upgrade.

Do not judge Lucio before completing his 5-star uncap and level 100. The pre-uncap version lacks the changes that restore his modern Full Auto value.

Lucio is **not an acquisition priority** for this account. He is strong after level 100, but Grand Sandalphon, Summer Horus and Nehan provide more important missing account functions.

## Current backline

### Default unattended Full Auto

1. **Yukata Zahlhamelina:** opening debuff immunity and 10% Dark-damage reduction work from reserve. This is the best broadly defensive owned passive.
2. **Yukata Narmaya**, when not frontline: every Light MC ougi adds 555,555 plain damage and 10% charge bar to MC.

Alternative:

- **Tikoh** replaces one slot when an extra All-Potion is useful and the player may intervene. The potion is added to inventory; Full Auto does not consume it automatically.
- If Narmaya is frontline, use Zahlhamelina plus Tikoh or the strongest emergency reinforcement.
- Grand Sandalphon eventually occupies a standard reserve slot whenever he is not frontline because his Primarch passive is more broadly valuable.

## Future Arcarum and Eternal roadmap

1. **Geisenborger / The Star:** Light's Evoker project. His reserve passive limits incoming Dark damage per hit, while his developed frontline form provides tanking and difficult-content utility. Farm The Star summon for Light damage progression even before the character is complete.
2. **Fif transcendence:** the safer Eternal project for casual long Full Auto and difficult content; prioritize only if the material burden is acceptable.
3. **Tweyen transcendence:** damage, debuff and hit-count specialist. She can help substitute for missing hit-count tools in specific content, but is not required merely to run daily Full Auto.

## Magna grid direction

Use **Luminiera Omega 250 × Luminiera Omega 250** as the default stable Full Auto setup.

General Magna III shell:

- 2–3 **Credo gun/Exalto** pieces as the modern offensive foundation;
- 1–2 **Credo spear/flex Magna III** pieces according to HP, cap and damage requirements;
- **Luminiera Sword Omega** pieces as transitional HP/attack filler, not an excuse to stop farming Magna III;
- **Magna Dark Opus**, eventually 5-star/6-star;
- **Ultima weapon** matching enough of the active frontline when appropriate;
- **Celestial weapon** matching the dominant frontline specialty;
- **Horus katana/Agastia or other farmable skill/CA pieces** only when the selected team actually benefits;
- **Draconic weapon** or defensive flex for bosses that kill the offensive shell.

For Team 1, prioritize normal-attack cap, supplemental damage and enough multiattack/HP to remain stable. For Team 2, prioritize CA/skill cap, supplemental skill damage and durability. Do not spend Damascus bars on farmable Magna pieces. Exact weapon counts must wait for the Light weapon payload.

## Acquisition priority

For this account and casual Full Auto:

1. **Grand Sandalphon:** highest broad account value because he combines frontline relevance with the Light Primarch passive.
2. **Summer Horus:** highest archetype-specific upgrade because Grand Cosmos is already owned. Her seasonal scarcity can make her the practical first spark when she is actually sparkable on a good 6% banner.
3. **Nehan:** strongest speed upgrade for Team 1, but lower urgency because Summer Payila + Basara + owned flex characters already produce a functional normal team.

This is not an absolute banner order. A proper Summer Horus spark opportunity should not be ignored merely to wait for a regular Gala target; conversely, do not chase her as an off-rate result. Nehan moves upward only if short farming speed becomes more important than general Full Auto coverage.

## Practical conclusion

- **Best owned fast core:** Summer Payila + Basara.
- **Current default casual preset:** Manadiver / Summer Payila / Basara / Grand Lu Woh, CA on.
- **Current fast/easy preset:** Manadiver / Summer Payila / Basara / Grand Jeanne, normally CA off.
- **Best owned Nehan substitute:** Grand Jeanne only for offense; Grand Lu Woh is the better general unattended-FA third member. No owned character duplicates Nehan exactly.
- **Long second preset:** Kengo / Grand Cosmos / Grand Noa / Grand Lu Woh.
- **Best current reserve passives:** Yukata Zahlhamelina and Yukata Narmaya; Tikoh is manual-intervention insurance.
- **First broad spark target:** Grand Sandalphon.
- **Seasonal opportunity target:** Summer Horus.
- **Nehan verdict:** excellent and still relevant, but no longer mandatory for this roster's casual Magna plan.
- **Lucio verdict:** not owned and not a spark priority; strong rather than bad at level 100, but redundant relative to higher-priority missing roles.
