# GBF Magna III Roadmaps: Fire, Wind, Light and Dark

Last researched: 2026-08-21

These grids are transition targets for the account's saved Team A, not copies of one-sided burst or Grand-heavy endgame examples. Each grid has ten main-grid slots and assumes elemental advantage.

## Fire

- Mainhand: Exo Maitrah Karuna, Attack awakening
- 3 Colossus Cane Ira
- 2 Colossus Bomber Ira
- Nilakantha
- Sol Remnant
- Scythe of Renunciation
- Sword of Valorblaze

Current Japanese guidance treats one to three Ira staves as the general-use boost core and one to two Ira axes as the double-sided skill-damage package. Five Ira staves are for specialized one-sided Extremity use. Nilakantha stays here because the account wants unattended Full Auto rather than a fragile burst sheet.

## Wind

- Mainhand: Tiamat Edge Aura
- 2 Tiamat Bolt Aura
- 2 Coruscant Crozier
- Ewiyar's Beak
- Innocent Love
- Spear of Renunciation
- Covenant-Ruin Fist
- Ring of Wandergale

Two Aura guns are the current ordinary-use target. Aura Edge provides Tempering, Garrison and a compatible Manadiver mainhand. Covenant-Ruin Fist matches Summer Mahira and Raphael in the saved primary team. This setup gets value from skill supplemental damage without adopting the separate three-gun/two-Aura-bow enmity burst grid.

## Light

- Mainhand: Exo Heliocentrum, Attack awakening
- 2 Luminiera Bolt Credo
- 3 Luminiera Sword Omega
- Luminiera Saber Credo
- Sword of Renunciation
- Altruism-Soul Staff
- Harp of Everlore

This is the durable two-Credo-gun transition. The five-gun grid requires Grand Sandalphon and is not the account's current roadmap. Exo Heliocentrum is a current farmable Manadiver staff that builds Light ATK and cap while charge bar is low, a natural fit for Manadiver's charge-bar consumption.

## Dark

- Mainhand: Dark Unsigned Kaneshige
- 3 Celeste Grace Ater
- 2 Celeste Saber Ater
- Zechariah
- Katana of Renunciation
- Altruism-Soul Staff
- Scythe of Darkherald

Three Ater fists and two Ater sabers are the current Magna III skill/stamina core. The mainhand is changed from the generic guide's dagger or staff to Kaneshige because the saved primary team is Kengo. Celestial Staff matches Grand Lich and Tsukuyomi in Team A and the staff-heavy Team B better than the generic Celestial Fist example.
