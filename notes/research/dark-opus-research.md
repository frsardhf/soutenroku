# Team A Dark Opus research

Research date: 21 August 2026. Scope: the primary Team A shown in `app/planner.tsx`, not a generic element-wide recommendation.

## Shared slot rule

Japanese player discussion generally keeps **Skill DMG Cap (Beta)** on Dark Opus and uses **Normal Attack Cap** on Ultima for normal or hybrid teams. This also strengthens the Dark Opus mainhand charge attack's additional damage. It is a slot pairing, not a claim that every team's largest damage category is skill damage.

Source: [Yahoo! Chiebukuro discussion of post-transcendence Opus/Ultima cap-key pairing](https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q13298321373)

## Fire — Manadiver / Grand Zeta / Michael / Grand Wilnas

- Confidence: medium; closest current composition, not an exact four-character match.
- Default: Beta + Pendulum of Strength.
- Transcended: Pendulum of Extremity.
- Short-only swap: Chain of Temptation.
- Evidence: a 2026 Fire Guild War player report repeatedly pairs `アビ` on Opus with `通常` on Ultima and uses `極破` for Manadiver/Grand Zeta normal teams. Michael appears as backline rather than frontline in that report, so the exact roster remains an inference.

Source: [2026 Fire Guild War composition notes](https://note.com/kyoka_h/n/ne28ec9360f7c)

## Water — Manadiver / Payila / Grand Gabriel / Grand Octavia

- Confidence: high for the key pairing and modes; the exact trio appears in 2026 Guild War reports, though not every source exposes every weapon image as text.
- Default general FA: Beta + Pendulum of Strength.
- Short Opus-mainhand rotation: Beta + Chain of Falsehood, usually with charge attacks off except for the setup charge attack.
- Transcended normal direction: Pendulum of Extremity.
- Evidence: current GameWith Water normal setups use `虚詐/アビ上限` on Opus with `通常攻撃上限` on Ultima. Japanese 2026 NM200 reporting with Gabriel and Octavia uses `極破` and documents their combined TA requirement.

Sources: [GameWith 2026 Water 90HELL setups](https://xn--bck3aza1a2if6kra4ee0hf.gamewith.jp/article/show/196333), [2026 Water NM200 report](https://note.com/nus7317/n/nc2f5868cd871), [2026 Water Guild War community notes](https://note.com/bcsuzuki/n/n01a0a5d4f537)

## Earth — Manadiver / Sabrina / Cidala / Yukata Pholia

- Confidence: medium-high for short content; no exact four-character published grid was found.
- Primary short FA: Beta + Chain of Falsehood.
- Longer-fight swap: Pendulum of Strength.
- Transcended: Pendulum of Extremity.
- Evidence: a Japanese Earth 95HELL guide recommends Falsehood for Manadiver reattack teams and separately names Sabrina and Yukata Pholia as strong normal/re-attack candidates. Published Cidala testing uses Extremity. Beta follows the common Opus/Ultima slot pairing and also covers Cidala's automatic skills.

Sources: [Earth 95HELL community guide](https://granbluefantasyblog.com/%E5%9C%9F%E5%8F%A4%E6%88%A6%E5%A0%B495hell1%E3%82%BF%E3%83%BC%E3%83%B3%E8%A8%8E%E4%BC%90%E7%B7%A8%E6%88%90%E9%9B%86%EF%BC%81/), [Cidala Full Auto showcase listing Extremity](https://yutura.net/channel/29055/video/LKbcBOxNgdY/)

## Wind — Manadiver / Summer Mahira / Grand Narmaya / Raphael

- Confidence: medium; Narmaya's normal-axis key pattern is established, but no exact current four-character guide was found.
- Fast content: Beta + Chain of Falsehood.
- General FA swap: Pendulum of Strength.
- Transcended: Pendulum of Extremity.
- Evidence: Japanese Wind Narmaya normal-axis material uses Skill Cap plus Falsehood on Opus and Normal Cap on Ultima. Narmaya is explicitly a normal-attack specialist, which supports Extremity. Summer Mahira and Raphael do not change the cap-slot non-duplication rule.

Sources: [Japanese normal-axis setup showing Opus Skill Cap/Falsehood and Ultima Normal Cap](https://eriscafe.com/granblue-20230907-ex-%EF%BD%83ocytus/), [Grand Narmaya normal-axis analysis](https://kirisamehare.com/gf_narumea_lim/)

## Light — Manadiver / Summer Payila / Basara / Grand Lu Woh

- Confidence: preliminary. Summer Payila released on 16 August 2026, so there is no mature exact-team guide yet.
- Default: Beta + Pendulum of Strength.
- Short-only swap: Chain of Temptation.
- Provisional transcended direction: Pendulum of Extremity.
- Evidence: early Japanese community discussion identifies Summer Payila as a normal-axis character. The local roster research likewise identifies Basara as a guaranteed-TA normal attacker. Beta is retained as the non-duplicating Opus slot while Normal Cap goes on Ultima. This must be rechecked after Light Guild War testing.

Sources: [Japanese release discussion with community comments](https://minigob-matome.blog.jp/archives/48816058.html), [Yahoo! community discussion four days after release](https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q11330599231)

## Dark — Kengo / Summer Azusa / Grand Lich / Tsukuyomi

- Confidence: medium-high for Beta; medium for Supremacy versus Sagacity.
- Second skill: Beta, not Gamma.
- Kengo default: Chain of Glorification; Pendulum of Strength is the safer general swap.
- Transcended: Pendulum of Supremacy while charge attacks lead; switch to Pendulum of Sagacity if the battle log shows Azusa/Lich skill damage leading.
- Evidence: current character testing specifically calls out the upgraded Skill Cap key's skill accuracy and cap as strong for Summer Azusa's 10-hit skill. A Japanese Magna example also uses Skill Cap + Stamina for Azusa. Her charge attack adds a large skill hit, while Lich adds recurring automatic skills, so Gamma would leave too much of the team unsupported.

Sources: [GameWith Summer Azusa mechanics and Opus interaction](https://xn--bck3aza1a2if6kra4ee0hf.gamewith.jp/article/show/390415), [Japanese Summer Azusa Magna example](https://sakon6172.com/2023yamikosenzyou-nikuatume1/)
