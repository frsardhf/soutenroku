# Revans account research

Last reviewed: 2026-09-13

This note records the evidence boundary behind the Revans pages. A composition
shown by a cited Japanese guide is **verified**. A mechanically plausible edit
made for the owned roster is an **account test** until it is run on self-hosts.
“Full Auto” is never used alone: host preparation, rescue contribution,
semi-Full Auto, and full-clear Full Auto are different activities.

## Account order

1. **Diaspora:** buildable now as a no-Okto CA-100 host handoff.
2. **Seofon:** closest Fire roster overlap, but the owned lineup is a self-host
   durability test rather than the verified Percival/Ragazzo rescue route.
3. **Agastia:** Grand Cosmos is owned; Pijiu is the practical entry gate.
4. **Mugen:** wait for initial Defense Schrodingers and Haaselia 5-star skill 4.
5. **Siegfried:** Cagliostro and Clarisse plus Grand Rosetta are owned, but the
   efficient modern control team still wants Estarriola.
6. **Cosmos:** last. The current Dark roster lacks a verified red/blue gauge
   control core and must not be represented as unattended-ready.

## Diaspora · Earth

- Use Kengo with Earth Unsigned Kaneshige, CA on, Full Guard off.
- Owned no-Okto host test: Aletheia / Earth Satyr / Holiday Anthuria, with Caim
  only when every equipped weapon name is unique.
- Reach CA activation 100%, stop at Emergency Repair System's 97-hit omen,
  request backup with `奥義100`, and do not attack further.
- The 13-slot all-unique grid follows the published farmable Magna shell. Its
  older Yggdrasil sword slot is upgraded to one Arbos sword for this account:
  Kaneshige, Arbos sword, Nibelung Klinge, Nibelung Horn, Ancient Perseus,
  Galleon's Jaw, Ished, Baihu Claw Malus, Gauntlet of Proudearth,
  Gateway-Star Sword, Binds of The Hanged Man, Tribunal Lyre Militis, and
  Pistol of Bahamut Coda.

Sources: [GameWith JP](https://xn--bck3aza1a2if6kra4ee0hf.gamewith.jp/article/show/322609),
[Otakupark CA-100 host](https://otakupark.com/deliasuporahl-kouryaku-ougi100nagasi-zihatuhuruo-tohennsei/),
and [Kamigame](https://kamigame.jp/%E3%82%B0%E3%83%A9%E3%83%96%E3%83%AB/%E3%82%AF%E3%82%A8%E3%82%B9%E3%83%88/%E3%83%9E%E3%83%AB%E3%83%81%E3%83%90%E3%83%88%E3%83%AB/%E3%83%87%E3%82%A3%E3%82%A2%E3%82%B9%E3%83%9D%E3%83%A9HL.html).

## Seofon · Fire

- Current short rescue references use Manadiver / Ragazzo / Grand Percival /
  Grand Zeta with CA off and roughly three to four turns.
- The owned Grand Zeta / Michael / Grand Wilnas version is a self-host test. It
  has no published guarantee of four million contribution or 10% survival.
- Open the raid before dealing four million damage so rescuers retain room.
  A defensive host needs enough HP to survive the 10% 77,777-damage trigger;
  current published host examples target roughly 100,000 HP.

Sources: [GameWith JP](https://xn--bck3aza1a2if6kra4ee0hf.gamewith.jp/article/show/390423),
[Otakupark](https://otakupark.com/grable-siete-hl-fully-automatic-formation-solo-relief-magna/),
and [Shiokalog 2026 self-host](https://shiokalog.com/gbf-seofon-hl-fullauto/).

## Agastia · Light

- CA is the accessible axis because repeated charge attacks work through the
  raid's severe per-hit reduction and build Limit Over.
- The current no-Summer-Horus reference uses Kengo / Grand Cosmos / Pijiu /
  Nehan, with CA on and Nehan skill 3 off. A Light Kaneshige is required.
- Grand Noa is an account substitution, not a verified Nehan replacement.
  Join near 100% while learning and test potion use plus the 60% 30-hit omen.

Sources: [GameWith JP](https://xn--bck3aza1a2if6kra4ee0hf.gamewith.jp/article/show/385099)
and [Otakupark](https://otakupark.com/agastya-hl-capture-full-auto-recover-as-appropriate-magna-formation/).

## Mugen · Water

- The 70% transition heals Mugen and permanently blocks normal healing, making
  barriers, mitigation, and cuts the real post-transition check.
- The owned Wamdus Kengo route is semi-Full Auto: alternate Wamdus CA and Fated
  Chain mitigation and manually call fire cut for Giant Ban after 70%.
- Routine defensive Full Auto is a later Paladin route using two to three
  Defense Schrodingers and Haaselia 5-star skill 4.

Sources: [GameWith JP](https://xn--bck3aza1a2if6kra4ee0hf.gamewith.jp/article/show/355009)
and [Otakupark](https://otakupark.com/granblue-fantasy-mugen-hl-strategy-solo-full-auto-rescue-formation-introduction-for-magna/).

## Siegfried · Wind

- Current Magna control references use Boogeyman / Estarriola / Cagliostro and
  Clarisse / Grand Rosetta, CA on, and Full Guard on. Pure Full Auto is slow,
  so the guide recommends reloads when contribution speed matters.
- The exact defensive grid target is Exo Australis mainhand, three Coruscant
  Croziers, three Tiamat Bolt Aura, two Tiamat Edge Aura, Spear of
  Renunciation, Melody of Judgement, Ultima Staff, and Altruism-Soul Staff.
- Estarriola is the efficient current target, not a historical hard gate;
  slower Black Cat, Monk, and Robin Hood defensive entries exist.

Sources: [Kamigame](https://kamigame.jp/%E3%82%B0%E3%83%A9%E3%83%96%E3%83%AB/%E3%82%AF%E3%82%A8%E3%82%B9%E3%83%88/%E3%83%9E%E3%83%AB%E3%83%81%E3%83%90%E3%83%88%E3%83%AB/%E3%82%B8%E3%83%BC%E3%82%AF%E3%83%95%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%83%88HL.html),
[Otakupark](https://otakupark.com/zi-kuhuri-to-mizugisionnnasi/), and
[2026 community Q&A](https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q14328016224).

## Cosmos · Dark

- Ten hits move the gauge toward blue; damage instances of at least three
  million move it toward red. Either extreme wipes the party.
- The current premium rescue reference is Manadiver / Summer Magus / Vikala /
  Indala with CA on and Indala skill 3 off. The cited Magna route joins near
  100%, opens with an All-Potion, and reloads in fast rooms.
- Summer Magisa is not Summer Magus. Lich can push blue, but the current owned
  Lich / Summer Magisa / Tsukuyomi team lacks a verified large-hit red
  counterweight. Keep this raid blocked rather than presenting a false FA.
